When you drop an image, you'll get a verdict, a score, and a look at the exact crop the model examined.

Close, front-facing face photos work best. Group shots and full scenes confuse a model that only learned from cropped faces.

A "real" result only means the model did not spot a StyleGAN fake. It does not prove a photo is genuine, and it cannot recognise other AI generators.

    How far to trust it

    These numbers were measured on images the model did not train on, from a single training run.

    Where it falls short

    • It only knows StyleGAN faces. Images from other generators, especially diffusion models, may slip through.
    • It works on a 64 x 64 crop, so fine detail is lost.
    • It overfits its training set, which is why it flags about one in six real faces by mistake.
    • Social-media robustness was only tested for JPEG compression, not resizing, screenshots or filters.
